🧪 Dataset & Architecture Specifications

  • Dataset Source: UCI Phishing Websites & PhishTank Corpus
  • Total Samples: 3,500 Balanced Vectors
  • Train / Test Split: 80% Training / 20% Testing (700 Holdout)
  • Feature Space: 16 Lexical, Structural & Cryptographic Features
  • Active Model Class: RandomForestClassifier
  • Hyperparameters: n_estimators=100, max_features='sqrt'
  • Inference Latency: ~12ms per URL scan
  • Model Serialization: Scikit-Learn Joblib Binary (.pkl)
